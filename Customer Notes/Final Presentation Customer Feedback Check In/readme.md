#### Liked
The clients were overall happy with the design. They especially liked the Health Suggestions and Testimonials. They thought it was a good idea to include the links to the sponsor's websites to improve Search Engine Optimization.

They also liked the flexibility of the Events, Resources, and Get Involved Pages. Those were set up in a way that allows for more pages to be added later.

#### Improvements need to make

During the presentation Angela wanted to know where the Facebook icon was. In the final design it appeared on the Blog page. However, it requires some searching to find and it would be better to also have it in the footer of every single page for easy access.

The white text on the banner was hard to see on certain pages. To increase the contrast, the opacity of the background overlay can be increased or have the background image replaced with one that offers more contrast.

The background images in the banner should be scaled so that they cover the entire page to avoid having to repeat the image to fill the space.

Some of the feedback was unsure about the mountains and landscape featured on the homepage and elsewhere on the site. Angela and Glenn both like it because of the connection to Andrew, but to the average user that is not clear. That connection should explained in Andrew's Journey and moves those images there, and replace them elsewhere, especially on the homepage.
